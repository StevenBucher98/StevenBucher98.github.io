About Steven Bucher

Product Manager at Microsoft | Innovation Advocate | PowerShell Enthusiast

My Mission

I'm passionate about innovation that creates positive impact for people, no matter what area I'm working in. Whether it's developing AI-powered tools, enhancing developer experiences, or exploring new technologies, my focus is always on building solutions that make a real difference in people's lives and work.

As a Product Manager at Microsoft, I have the privilege of working on tools that help developers and IT professionals be more productive and creative. I graduated from Santa Clara University and am early in my career, driven by curiosity and the belief that technology should empower and uplift everyone.

What Drives Me

I believe the best innovations come from understanding real problems and iterating quickly to find practical solutions. I'm energized by:

  • Impact-driven work: Building tools and experiences that make a measurable, positive difference
  • Continuous learning: Exploring emerging technologies and sharing knowledge through presentations and projects
  • Human-centered design: Ensuring technology serves people, not the other way around
  • Collaborative innovation: Working with diverse teams to create solutions that matter

Beyond Work

When I'm not working on product features or speaking at conferences, I enjoy experimenting with creative projects, contributing to open source, and finding new ways to blend technology with art and everyday life.
